Hello, good morning, good afternoon, and goodnight. Welcome to my website (or just a plain webpage). I am 藤宮天音 (Fujimiya Amane, or あまね for short), i.e. Bui Gia Khanh (Vietnamese name). I am a major in Physics, Mathematics and Computer Science in Department of Physics, Hanoi University of Science, college of Vietnam National University. For my current works, please see here.

My main field of interest includes philosophy (more on system philosophy, like Heideggerian philosophy), physics (theoretical physics, foundational physics and quantum optics of which I am researching in), theoretical aspects of sciences — for example, theoretical machine learning, theoretical artificial intelligence (a broader term, while still exists, is partially faded into the history more or less), theoretical computer science, and something else.

Currently, my works include researching on the topic of double descent in theoretical consideration of machine learning, Raman spectroscopy with machine learning-aid application, and theoretical analysis on nonlinear absorption coefficients of quantum well in setting of semiconductor structures. I also have plans for multiple things include online books or research documentary, though that remains to be seen.

Collaboration is gratefully welcome, since I do not have much thing to do anyway. Probably have to make the spare part goes away. For more information, please see the about page.
