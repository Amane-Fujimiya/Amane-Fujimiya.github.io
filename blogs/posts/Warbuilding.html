Star Trek, to Star Wars; from Battlestar Galactica, to sci-fi military world-building. From fictions to fictions, we have been trying to depict wars, depicting military structures, and depicting different kind of command structure, or institution. While stories of such, and their focus on the individuals, the actors, the heroes, and so on, are fantastic and spectacular, they fail in one certain aspect. The depiction of survival, the depiction of an organic system, the depiction of existence of a typical institution. The depiction at most, of constraints, within those parameters, without magic, without Deus ex machina, without god-giving grace. Boiling down to technical domain, everything simply sounds fake. Intents, personalities, individuals leakage in places where one expects actual order. Coldness in wrong tensor, personal in wrong consequence. Such is the failing grace of what can be observed from latent media or fictions of which we have seen, though one can forbid such incompleteness and focus on the things they did best - drawing out a world of fiction and stories related to the actors. But we want a different thing. So let us try doing that, this time, through a kind of story: The cold, hard, monotonic facet of reports.

The timeline

The timeline of this is rather simple, and I take particular freedom of intersection in which to write the stories while interacting with it in Aurora 4x. You know, the game in which you are basically NORAD UI, with spreadsheet. Fortunately, it gives you far more than it should of the freedom permitted, and we might as well utilize that to great effect. The timeline will be clarified later, and we for now will interface with it directly.

Phase I

In lore, phase I is where we would say, options and operations are standing, and effectively, implicitly, peacetime. We assume the interstellar empire to be a constitutional monarchy, with Senate, High Council, Civilian Oversight Committees, and Supreme High Command, or HIGHCOM separately on particular lower-order directive framing. Reorganization starts from 2050, and the world is with accelerated tech, meaning they are far more advanced than we are currently right now to reach this level by then.

By 2053, we have another topic on naval reorganization. Of course, the ground force would not be able to organize in such manner that fast, so we expect also, a tail-heavy, thin-tower hierarchy of ground force across command, with PODCOM dominating in logistics. But let’s see why CCHC035 is required. From what we can infer,

  • The civilization does not require ground assets far more than garrison, and so on. That is why it is reorganized into institutionalized PODCOM, standing for Planetary, Orbital Defence Command, handling both planetary and orbital security near natural mass concentrations, i.e. planets, comets, mining colonies, moons, and so on.
  • Before this, RCJF and ROC are two operational commands under special designation, under PODCOM. Their classification is thus by law, lower by one priority, for PODCOM being R1, they would be R2 and below. Freeing this options means a majority of Senate and Crown request for elevation and independent of central government, which, implies something must have happened, because it is politically costly, though not impossible, but the cost is often irreversible within such institutional monarchy system.
  • The scope also changed. As said, PODCOM used to handle only ‘coursework’ on planetary support. In such, we can say the system implies fear of coup d’tat, such that naval asset in deep space, which heavily relies on logistics, to be not independent of planetary governance, hence civilians, to be unable to act against the civilian government without two-way cost. Thus, implies the change stems from certain knowledge of condition for the political structure to acknowledge elevation of force creation, so is to create effective ground force after abolition from a rather complicated history pre-unification.

Such can be said why the directive is formed. However, this move is organizational heavy, thus bring about the next issue, “On naval reorganization and standing order”, due February 2053.

Here, we have a set of military priority designation on order or decision templates, starting of R1 to R6. Civilian authority, as expected of institutional, civilian-dominated government structure, requires specification on civilian priority, specified via CIVPRI-Y for Y ranging from A to F. Most of the time, it is used for classification and categorization of assets or relational actions, but we bracketed the entire civilian government to such interface via CIVGOV and designated code of communication; this is because we focus on the military’s POV, which makes sense. However, two actors are there to restrict what they do, and must be intact in purpose of constraints: the civilian government, as said, and the Royal Family, with a collection of political and noble entities. That said, since the messages are from R1 to R2, we would not be hearing from said actors many times, but more so the Royal Family substantial force projection and crown defence. Accordingly, when CIVPRI is specified for operational purpose and not order document designation, we use them as code band A/F, where one of their most important identification is the degree of information specificity. Simply speak, code band A is where the entire central government must be informed immediately, bypassing scheduled meetings and hearing and push to, realistically in our world, priority one message, so are for decision. We reserve R0 for absolute bypass. This is only required in very specific case basis, and is rather extremely heavy. In essence, it is an overriding option, so much similar to CCHC035, but to a given time frame, it overrides priorities for all active class. Similar thing goes for CIVPRI-A, however, options on elevation to R0-equivalent, CIVPRI-O, lies in the hand of the Head of State, in conjunction with the Crown, and partial majority in both Parliament and the Low Senate.

Separating from such, the codes are rather simple. For example, CCHC means Central Command, High Command. If, for example, the order is from CCNV, we says it is of Central Command, Naval (Command). HIGHCOM is the only designation with abbreviation on central command with classification explicit, because it contains HIGHCOM-A in tangent of Supreme High Command (SHC), and HIGHCOM-B for another document classification of CO, by Command Operation. In fact, Supreme High Command can be considered a Joint Force Command Structure (JFCS), in which they are the combination of CIVGOV high-order communication pathway, special operations and separated operational entities, HIGHCOM, and INTELCOM, though nominally INTELCOM is under SHC.

So, let us recall of the structure that is present at the moment. The principle of the reorganization is the acceptance that “Any concentration of force will eventually try to optimize for itself.”, thus making two directive of reorganization as being forced hands. On one side, you remove legible security against forced coup d’tat, and on the other side, there exists pressure that change the dynamic in such way. Granted, there exists no available internal pressure stemming from civil war issues, social issues and so on large enough to justify a structural change in Army composition. Such is obvious as to extend PODCOM only to be a rational mode in such issue, not directly removing a barrier set precedent from constitution. Thus, it means that there exists external pressure, one that is large enough, but also ambiguous enough to force changing hand, from both the Crown and indirectly majorative wing of constitutional and formed nobles influence, civilian government with majority on Senate, and overriding constitutional organization means alignment of also all High Council, Parliament, and within all civilian oversight and independent watchdogs. Justifying such move is done by all parties, thus pertaining to shared cost, and not sided responsibility.

Phase II

Phase two will be different. We will go through series of reports, before doing anything of analysis. So let us gather them up. The date is 2056, i.e. within reorganization time frame, there exists a six-year gap. Just to be clear, the minimum depth of the Empire, spanning from Sol axis, is 41LY, so for anything going inside such, for example, Proxima Centauri, condition protocol elevates to R1-capable order. We assume jump points or FTL jump-capable assets. Lastly, we introduce the concept of condition directive, in real world can be analogous to DEFCON, but here, we set them apart a bit. Functionally, there are,

  • SEC-CON-X for X from 1 to 5. This is operational condition code, specified for active alert condition. Used for operations and active movement interdiction, i.e. anything that requires sector concentration. Limits to sector governance of all sub-districts.
  • UNV-CON-X for X from 1 to 4. This is again, operational condition code, but differs in scope range for all legible territory under control or partial control. Alert status on latent frame.
  • SEC-DEFCON-Q for Q from A to E. This is operational protocol condition code, i.e. we can say as the background addition level specifiable for protocols. It will enact specific double measure or heightened procedures during condition alert. Use in separation to the above two codes, but usually comparable.
  • UNV-DEFCON-Q is the same, but for universal condition, not sector-based.

This is the basic lingua franca for the section itself.

Analysis

So, the situation simulates first-contact scenario on procedure. However, while most scenarios would be giving such in the stance of border contact, or exploration contact, here, we shift to core contact. As specified, Proxima Centauri is one of many Core Systems, not Inner Colonies or Outer Colonies. This marks the situation as penetration in depth, and breach of critical level. That is why per usual, procedure concurs the use of R2 shift and request, but immediately CIVPRI-A and R1 is enacted, by severity and so is SEC-CON-1. The condition code is conservative, speaking from position of Admiral Naoki. However, it implies larger attention, since sector defence, condition code 1 means that it has potential for near-sector code 2. If such situation is not resolved, UNV-CON-2 at least follows, and right now, that code is not on the border, but from the elevated core. Thus justified the reaction.

Secondly, is the ineffectiveness of combat. While it is not true that the hostile force, or REDFOR, has the total damage upper hand or offensive capacity advantage, what they have is shielding, implicitly, and manoeuvring capacity. Such allows for unrestricted paths or attack vector, thus renders prediction and orbital \(\Delta V\) ineffective, thus losing tactical leverage. Couple that with near-peer level of offensive capacity, and superior EWAR capacity, results in later total collapse of operational effectiveness of 4th Fleet. They are not totally destroyed, however, since Admiral Naoki escaped while relaying position of enemy, thus allowing SHC to specify breach range and direct Melbridge Cosmic Belt defensive posture, which is situated between Proxima Centauri and other inner system colonies. 100+ AU is the section length, i.e., by Empire doctrine, each defensive line in space must be specified by at least two parameters: depth \(d\) on minimum attack vector prevention, and length \(\ell\) for maximal deviation from standard vector, i.e. orthorgonal plane coverage. The actual defensively is the entire Melbridge Belt, with extension to cover almost all angles. This is also because upon realization, as we have seen,

All remaining sectors expect invasion force moving from bearing 225, all angle, all range.

This includes the acceptance of condition that hostile force has potentially unlimited mobility. Under such condition, defensively cannot be simply provided under non-umbrella posture, and must be full coverage by priority of core systems. However, this leads to disorganization made by rapid attack and surprised vector destruction, so the forces are thinned out, and part of the line was exposed, and hostile force punched through this.

After punching through, the force goes straight for vector toward Sol System. Upon not knowing which vector in specific would it take, whether Earth or anywhere, HIGHCOM realizes that aside from this extremely strong vector, the threat still remains, but are small or conditionally focused. Thus, the order is to remain umbrella, and gather a substantial deployment force, priority R1 to defend Sol, while also keeping other Outer Colonies and further sectors with warnings and communication of elevated condition status. In further notice, where the threat is clear, and enemy breach Inter-Sol and various defensive layers of the system themselves to Earth, HIGHCOM issued the final order conjunction, which says basically,

Expect hostile vectors from all bearing, all angle, all range, no prediction available. All traffic R2 and below not permitted to Sol Sector (to prevent casualties under existential threat), but allows for earlier R1 detachment to enter the system for reinforcement.

The strategy is to force a dual pinning on the enemy. Sol system has Gravity Well, which distort Jump capacity, of which was deployed en-mass in the previous CCNV093-C and CCNV093-Q where Melbridge was breached. The pincer movement is to force time, and to learn more about what the hostile force wants. Under the same line, implicitly mentioned, SHC and CIVGOV also enacted protocol Alpha, which enables activation of a deactivated unless conditions are met, CIVPRI-O, for MOTCOM, or Mothership Command. This is existential crisis, thus allows for activation of such force. Fortunately, the adversary did retreat, though that does not change their posture for the longer duration of the defence and buildup phase.

What can we learn about this part is not much. The situation is already laid out as said. However, the narrative is clear. This is a total shock to the entire military, though much are anticipated beforehand ever since ground reorganization. In fact, most gravity wells are concentrated on Earth’s surface sections, and was defended against orbital detachments from spaces by hostile forces. They are marginally equivalent in strength, however with contested and often superior orbital control, assets on ground of REDFOR has the upper hand if and only if PODCOM was retained. The decision to actualize GRPCOM was then evaluated as a right decision despite its dangers, and here is perhaps why the decision while of good faith, is irreversible. Still, it was a success.

There exists a part of few designation that would be of interest. For example, the simple document class and subclass. We have been seeing this ever since the first record, however, what do they mean? Well, realistic military also has its own share of class enumeration and tagging, but there are a few general one. For example, Mn for maintainance, M for military, as certain records are from CIVGOV, or coauthored of such, N is Naval, G is Ground, A is for Active (meaning considering active operations or conditions), I for intelligence, R is for Readjustment (can overlap with Ne, for Neuausrichtung, or FR for force readjustment). Aside from that, technical section is contained in parts, for example,

Sector jamming is in effect, time to estimated deployment of 5 unit, angle 335, comm band A, degree G, offset 44FA+, Base frequency 123000+5. Counter-jamming is in effect, with 3 unit strength lift, run-time class A4. Expect total blindness. Gravitational blockage is deployed, ETA 33 Standard Unit Time.

We have been seeing coordinates. Let us start with it first. Nominally, three-dimensional warfare, ignoring cost topology, and geometric terrains, we have a set of three-parameter focal to take care of, being \[ \mathbf{r} = (b,[\theta_{x},\theta_{y},\theta_{z}],r) \] This stems from probabilistic and Keplerian orbit. Basically, for each \(dr\), we need to specify the orbital plane that the path is on. This requires plane alignment, i.e. bearing \(b\). Furthermore, since orbital difference is measured of angle of path elevation, conditioning on the Galatic Centre or Earth Centre axis, the angle is calculated relative to said axis, and expressed by the three-\(\theta\) treatment. Finally, radial orbital \(\Delta V\) manoeuvre accepts motion cost by default, thus most navigation requires vector-free distance. This is the standard computational doctrine of the Imperial Navy. With such, you can perhaps derive why they failed during combat records, of which if released, would be able to simulate the superiority in movements.

Now, for the technical part. There is this technique in technical writing and realism forcing, that to be world-aware and context-heavy. Think about it a bit. This is an early-stage, semi-mature interstellar empire, spanned after a unification war implicitly, and of constitutional monarchy system. They will, obviously, have different standards, EWAR techs, encryption schemes, information carrier specification different from what we currently have, for example, using optical system instead, or quantum Q-224 encryption scheme. The system is mature, and there are things that would be taken for granted, because they are not simply naive and starting from zero. Such is to say, the documents are specified by insiders, for insiders. The writing by then, would like to be referencible by design dimension. That is, what should be written down to express (what kind of information matters), and not actual metric. Or simply speak, give information density and interpretability, not ultra-specific technical specification, because when writing of a far-flung future, it is future for a reason, and furthermore, to work inside a machine, not to operate like a narrator outside of it. Functionally real internal documents never explain their own infrastructure. Aside from scientific showdown, most scenario when writing such, degrades the individual in question for incompetence, in-universe awkward and rather impotent, and many if not all to simply be a wasteful timing of relevance and so on. Listing wavelengths, algorithms, or quantum jargon would actually make the text less believable, because real operational writing never does that outside of manuals, so to speak, so stop bringing them in. At least, do it in the footnote.
